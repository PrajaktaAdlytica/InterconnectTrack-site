Questions before an evaluation.

Is this a grid study?

No. InterConnectTrack is a research-stage concept for read-only evidence support. Accredited engineering, utility/ISO studies and approval remain outside its authority.

Which regions and rules are supported?

The prototype has one explicitly synthetic example. Real jurisdiction-specific workflows will only be demonstrated after the actual dated rules are loaded and reviewed.

What is connected to this demo?

Nothing external. It uses a fixed invented queue event log and mock project records. There is no dispatch, utility writeback or production connector.

What would a two-site evaluation need?

Two previously screened sites, authorized redacted queue/study evidence, a named engineer, a historical comparator, a pre-agreed rubric and conditional paid SOW.

Are the proposed prices final?

No. Each price is an interview-test hypothesis and a separate scope discussion, not an accepted market rate or active checkout.

Who owns the decision?

A named developer-side engineer reviews consequential assumptions. The product does not approve a connection, certify capacity or make physical/control decisions.
